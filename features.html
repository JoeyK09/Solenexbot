Site

Landing pages & link-in-bio

Publish a link-in-bio page for your socials, or a full landing page with a hero, feature sections, and a call to action — live at your own URL.

List products with a price, description, and image. Buyers check out with M-Pesa, PayPal, or crypto, and get their delivery info — a download, a code, instructions — the moment they pay.
